# Licensing

This repository holds three kinds of work under three different terms. The
`LICENSE` file at the root covers the **source code only** — read this page
for the full picture before reusing anything else.

| What | Where | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| Source code | `src/`, `scripts/`, build and config files | [MIT](LICENSE) |
| Data and research prose | `data/`, `docs/` | [CC BY-SA 4.0](data/LICENSE) |
| Audio | `public/audio/*.mp3` | © 2026 Mehmet Fatih Aksoy. All rights reserved. |
| Third-party material | throughout | Their originators' terms — see [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md) |

## Source code — MIT

Everything that makes the atlas run: the Next.js application, the WebGL galaxy
and map renderers, the layout engine, the validators and the build scripts.
Standard MIT terms, in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## Data and research prose — CC BY-SA 4.0

The sourced mythology layer and the research behind it: characters, relations,
stories, places, lineages and cultural records under `data/`, and the source,
character and contradiction catalogs under `docs/`.

ShareAlike here is an obligation inherited from upstream, not a preference.
`data/reference/*.json` adapts Wikipedia articles (CC BY-SA 4.0) and
`data/geo/basemap.json` adapts a Wikimedia Commons SVG map series
(CC BY-SA 3.0); neither can be relicensed under permissive terms by anyone,
including the copyright holder of the surrounding work. River geometry derived
from OpenStreetMap carries its own ODbL 1.0 terms on top.

Full terms and attribution guidance: [data/LICENSE](data/LICENSE).

**If you reuse a fact, keep its `sources` and `citation` fields with it.** They
are the point of the dataset: every mythological claim records which ancient
authors attest it, and stripping that turns sourced research back into
unattributed assertion.

## Audio — all rights reserved

`public/audio/*.mp3` — the ambient bed and the four Odyssey voyage stems —
were generated by the author with Suno under a paid plan. They ship here so
the application runs from a clean checkout; they are not licensed for reuse,
redistribution or derivative works, and are covered by neither the MIT nor the
CC BY-SA grant above.

## What is not in this repository

The ancient primary texts the research rests on are **not** redistributed.
`research/corpus/manifest.json` pins twenty editions by URL, edition
statement, license and SHA-256 checksum; `pnpm corpus:sync` fetches them into
a local corpus that is gitignored. Their licenses are their originators' own.

## Attribution

[ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md) names every upstream source — Perseus,
ToposText, Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons, Wikidata, Pleiades, Natural Earth,
OpenStreetMap, Mapterhorn, astronomy-engine and the fonts — with its license
and what was taken from it. Attribution errors are treated as bugs; please
open an issue if you find one.
