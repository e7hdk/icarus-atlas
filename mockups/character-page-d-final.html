Unchanged by the lens above — how the last twenty-eight centuries have seen him.

Zeus is the sky and thunder god of ancient Greek religion, king of the gods on Mount Olympus. His great sanctuary at Olympia hosted the games held in his honor, and the colossal gold-and-ivory statue of him there, sculpted by Phidias, was counted among the Seven Wonders of the ancient world.
