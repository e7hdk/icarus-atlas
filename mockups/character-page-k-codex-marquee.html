⁂

The legendary son of Zeus and Danaë. With the help of the gods, he beheaded the Gorgon Medusa, rescued Andromeda from a sea monster, and founded the great city of Mycenae.1

I

The chest on the waves

Born in a subterranean bronze chamber after Zeus visited his mother Danaë in a shower of gold, Perseus was cast into the sea in a wooden chest by his paranoid grandfather Acrisius. He washed ashore on Seriphus, where he was raised by the fisherman Dictys.2

II

The Gorgon quest

To protect his mother from the unwanted advances of King Polydectes, Perseus embarked on a seemingly impossible quest to retrieve the head of the Gorgon Medusa. Guided by Hermes and Athena, he acquired winged sandals, a magic wallet, and the cap of Hades ⚖. He beheaded Medusa and used her petrifying gaze to turn his enemies — the titan Atlas and the sea monster Cetus among them — to stone.3

III

Return & Mycenae

After rescuing and marrying the Aethiopian princess Andromeda, he returned to Greece. Fulfilling an ancient prophecy, he accidentally killed his grandfather Acrisius with a stray discus. Ashamed to take the throne of Argos, he exchanged kingdoms with his cousin Megapenthes and founded the mighty fortress of Mycenae.4
