## 1. Why this dynasty

1. **It is the atlas's central knot.** Heracles ties the monster bestiary (as their slayer), Calydon (Deianira), Arcadia (Auge/Telephus, Echemus), Sparta (the Heraclid Return), the Perseid house, Thebes (Megara), and the Argo into one figure.
2. **It activates dozens of dormant edges.** Seven monster-foes already hang in the chthonic quarter waiting for a slayer; the Heraclid-Return milestone (PLAN §M11) unlocks once Heracles lands.
3. **It completes the Perseid bridge.** Perseus, Andromeda, Danae and Gorgophone are in; the Perseid kings of Mycenae/Tiryns (Electryon, Alcaeus, Sthenelus, Mestor) and Eurystheus the taskmaster join them, and through Electryon → Alcmene reach Heracles.
4. **Its cardinal dispute is foundational.** Heracles' paternity (Zeus vs Amphitryon) is the archetypal divine/mortal double-fatherhood.

## 7. Relation & data-modeling notes

1. `heracles-father` = competing topic-tagged parent edges `heracles`→`zeus` and `heracles`→`amphitryon`; mother `heracles`→`alcmene` undisputed. The twins `heracles`↔`iphicles` share the mother (a sibling edge despite the divine/mortal fathers — the twin bond).
2. The Perseid sons take `parent`→`perseus` and `parent`→`andromeda`, joining `gorgophone` in a sibling clique.
3. The Labours: model `heracles` as `slayer` of the killed beasts (Nemean Lion, Hydra, Geryon, Ladon, the Stymphalian birds, the Mares, the Hesperian dragon) and `adversary` of the captured-alive ones (Ceryneian Hind, Erymanthian Boar, Cretan Bull, Cerberus, Hippolyta) — choose per source; `heracles` adversary `eurystheus`.
4. Heracles is the boundary frame the monster batches deferred — those labour-kills now become real edges.
5. Cluster: the human house → `mortal-arm`; the new labour-beasts (Erymanthian Boar, Ceryneian Hind, Stymphalian Birds) → `mortal-arm` (regional beasts, like the existing `cretan-bull`); Nessus/Pholus the centaurs → `mortal-arm` (like `chiron`); Hebe → the divine band; Diomedes-of-Thrace/Augeas/Hippolyta → `mortal-arm`.
6. No lineage/residences file (Heracles is pan-Hellenic; Tiryns/Thebes are his frames, not a single seat).

## 9. Conclusion

Heracles is the hinge of the whole heroic age and the keystone the atlas has been building toward: a Perseid by blood (through Electryon to Alcmene), a son of Zeus by the cardinal double-fatherhood, the slayer who at last gives the chthonic bestiary its hunter, the husband of Deianira and father of Telephus and Hyllus, and the one mortal taken up to Olympus to wed Hebe. Build the Perseid bridge and the birth first (Batch A), the Twelve Labours that activate the dormant monster-edges second (Batch B), the wives, the death on Oeta, and the eldest Heraclids third (Batch C); resolve the `perses`/`sthenelus`/`iphicles`/`diomedes`/`eurytus` homonyms up front, keep the Dorian Return for its own batch, and preserve the paternity of Heracles and of Augeas as first-class disputes.
