# The Calydonian / Aetolian House — Aetolus to Diomedes — Research Dossier

> Research status: source-mapped against the seven atlas lenses (corpus + web), 2026-06-13. **House complete, entered 2026-06-14.** Batch A (M2.7) = 14 figures, 35 relations, three contradictions (`meleager-father`, `deianira-parentage`, `thestius-parentage`); Batch B/C (M2.8) = 6 figures (the Thestiads Iphiclus/Plexippus, Cleopatra, Periboea, Agrius, and the river-god Achelous), 21 relations, one contradiction (`tydeus-mother`). 20 figures total; all ship the three-layer codex. The collateral names Melas and Andraemon stay note-only (referenced in prose, not promoted). Verified branch-by-branch; same-name hazards and existing-node collisions resolved before entry (per CLAUDE.md hard rule 7). Homonym-prone ids suffixed defensively: `plexippus-thestiad` (≠ Phineus' son), `agrius-aetolian` (≠ the Giant / centaur / Odysseus' son).
>
> Scope: the Aetolian house from Aetolus (already in the atlas, the Aeolid son of Endymion) through the city-eponyms Pleuron and Calydon to Oeneus of Calydon, the Calydonian Boar Hunt and Meleager, and the line of Tydeus down to Diomedes. Heracles (Deianira's husband) and the Theban war are existing/boundary frames the house plugs into.

## 1. Why this dynasty needs a dossier

1. **Its root is already in the sky.** `aetolus` was entered with the Aeolids (son of Endymion, grandson of Calyce). The eponyms Pleuron and Calydon hang off him — wire onto the existing node.
2. **It bridges to Sparta.** Thestius is the father of both Althaea (Calydon) and the existing `leda` (Sparta) — adding Thestius wires `leda` to her father and joins the two houses.
3. **It feeds two of the great cycles.** Meleager and the boar gather the heroes of Greece; Tydeus joins the Seven against Thebes (with the existing `polynices`), and his son Diomedes is a first-rank Iliad hero who wounds the existing `aphrodite` and `ares`.
4. **Severe homonyms with existing nodes.** Agenor (Phoenician vs Aetolian), Periboea (Icarius' wife vs Oeneus' wife), Cleopatra (the Boread vs Meleager's wife), Diomedes (the Argive hero vs the Thracian horse-tamer).
5. **The signature myths fork.** Meleager dies by his mother's brand or in the Curetes' war; Deianira and Meleager each have a divine-paternity variant (Dionysus, Ares); Atalanta's own parentage is disputed.

## 8. Relation & data-modeling notes

1. `parent` child→parent only; the divine-paternity forks (`meleager-father`, `deianira-parentage`, `thestius-parentage`) are competing topic-tagged parent edges on one child.
2. Atalanta typed `hero` (the huntress), entered here as the boar-hunt guest; her full Arcadian saga (footrace, golden apples) is a later batch, with `atalanta-parentage` already flagged.
3. Diomedes' woundings of gods are `adversary` edges (`diomedes`→`aphrodite`, `diomedes`→`ares`), not `slayer` (the gods are immortal).
4. No lineage file/residences: Calydon and Pleuron are regions, not the six flagship cities.
5. Boundary non-nodes (prose only): Heracles, Nessus, Adrastus, Melanippus, Odysseus, the Lycian Glaucus.

## 10. Conclusion

The Calydonian house is the Aetolian hinge of the heroic age: from the eponym Aetolus already in the sky descend Oeneus' wine, the great boar and Meleager's fatal brand, the Sparta-bridging Thestius, and the line of Tydeus that produces Diomedes, the mortal who wounded two gods. Build the spine first (Batch A), wiring the root `aetolus`, the bridge `leda`, and the Iliad/Theban membrane; add the Thestiads and inner circle second; the collateral Aetolians (and the river-god Achelous) third. Resolve every homonym up front, keep Heracles and the Theban Melanippus as boundary prose, and preserve each divine-paternity fork as a first-class dispute.
