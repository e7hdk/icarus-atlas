## 3. Homonym hazards

| Name | Figures | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Daphne | Peneus' daughter → laurel (Ovid, Hyginus) vs Arcadian huntress on the Ladon (Pausanias 8.20.2–4) | Separate prose note in `daphne-laurel`; do not merge under one node |
| Eurydice | Orpheus' wife (Ovid, Apollodorus) vs Nestor's wife `eurydice-nestor` vs Theban `eurydice-thebes` | Plain-name cast in Orpheus episodes; no merged character node |
| Pygmalion | Sculptor who loved his statue (Ovid) vs king of Cyprus, father-in-law of Cinyras (Apollodorus) | Plain-name cast in `pygmalion`; royal Pygmalion named only in Apollodorus chapter |
| Adonis | Born from myrrh-tree after incest (Ovid) vs son of Cinyras and Metharme (Apollodorus) | `myrrha-adonis` ch. Cinyras' house carries the dispute in prose |
| Erysichthon | Thessalian glutton who felled Ceres' oak (Ovid Met. 8) vs childless son of Cecrops (Apollodorus 3.14.2) | Plain-name cast in `erysichthon-hunger`; Cecrops' son named only in homonym note |
| Scylla | Nisus' daughter of Megara (Ovid, Apollodorus) vs the six-headed monster of the straits (`scylla-monster`) | `scylla-minos` uses `scylla-nisus` node; never merge with the Homeric sea-beast |
| Galatea | Sea-nymph loved by Acis (Ovid Met. 13–14) vs post-Ovid name for Pygmalion's statue in `pygmalion` | Plain-name nymph in `acis-galatea`; ivory bride stays unnamed in Pygmalion episode |
