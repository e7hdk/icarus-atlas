# The Divine Family — the Muses, Graces, Seasons & the lesser gods — Research Dossier

> Research status: source-verified against the seven atlas lenses (Hesiod *Theogony* the master source), 2026-06-14, corpus-first. Gates the divine-family batch (M2.21). Same-name hazards resolved per CLAUDE.md hard rule 7 BEFORE the batch was written.

## 1. Why this batch

The atlas held the twelve Olympians and their major children, but the *retinue* of the divine family was missing: the Muses, the Graces, the Seasons, the messenger Iris, the childbirth-goddess Eileithyia, the cross-roads goddess Hecate, and the four winged powers who stand by Zeus's throne. This batch enters them, and ties up the loose end left by the primordial work — Styx's children (Nike, Zelus, Cratos, Bia), named in the Styx entry but not yet stars.

## 2. Roster (22 figures)

- **The nine Muses** (Zeus + Mnemosyne, Theog. 53–79, 915–917): `calliope`, `clio`, `euterpe`, `erato-muse`, `melpomene`, `polyhymnia`, `terpsichore`, `thalia-muse`, `urania`. (type god, cluster olympian-band)
- **The three Charites / Graces** (Zeus + the Oceanid Eurynome, Theog. 907–911): `aglaea`, `euphrosyne`, `thalia-charis`.
- **The three Horae / Seasons** (Zeus + Themis, Theog. 901–906): `eunomia`, `dike`, `eirene`.
- **Hecate** (Perses + Asteria, Theog. 409–411; type god, cluster chthonic), **Iris** (Thaumas + the Oceanid Electra, Theog. 265–266), **Eileithyia** (Zeus + Hera, Theog. 922).
- **The children of Pallas & Styx** (Theog. 383–385): `nike`, `zelus`, `cratos`, `bia` — Zeus's throne-guard.

## 3. Same-name hazard map & collisions (resolved)

| Name | Resolution |
|---|---|
| **Erato** | the Muse is `erato-muse` — the EXISTING `erato-dryad` (Arcas' wife) must not be touched. |
| **Thalia** | the Muse of comedy is `thalia-muse`; the Grace is `thalia-charis` — two distinct new nodes for the one shared name. |
| **Clio** | the Muse `clio` ≠ the Oceanid Clio (Theog. 364, not a node). |
| **Nike / Dike / Aglaea / Eunomia / Eirene / Bia / Cratos / Zelus** | all free ids, no existing collision. |
| **Eurynome / Electra (Oceanid)** | the mothers of the Charites and of Iris are NOT nodes — kept in prose, no dangling edge (matching how the existing Harpies are wired to Thaumas only). |

## 5. Cross-links into the standing sky

- `aglaea` consort `hephaestus` (the Hesiodic marriage, against the Homeric Aphrodite — topic `hephaestus-consort`).
- `iris` sibling the existing `harpies`, ally `hera` (her messenger role).
- `eunomia` adversary the existing `dysnomia` (Good Order vs Lawlessness — Hora vs strife-child).
- `sirens` gain their disputed Muse-mothers (`melpomene` / `terpsichore`) atop their existing father `achelous`.
- `hyacinthus` gains the Apollodoran variant parent `clio` (joining the existing `hyacinthus-parentage` dispute).
- `nike`/`zelus`/`cratos`/`bia` ally `zeus` (the Titanomachy throne-guard).

## 6. Notes

- The individual Muses' specific arts (epic, history, dance…) are a late Roman/Hellenistic systematization, presented as such, not as early-source fact.
- All 22 ship the three-layer codex; 63 verified Wikimedia-Commons artworks across the batch (only `zelus`, pure Zeal, carries an honest empty Legacy).
