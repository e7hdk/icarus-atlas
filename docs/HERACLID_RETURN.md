## 1. Why this batch

1. **It closes the Heracles arc.** Heracles' line, persecuted by Eurystheus, at last reclaims the Peloponnese — the historical "Dorian" charter myth the Spartans and Argives lived by.
2. **It binds the Pelopid and Tegean houses.** The Heraclids drive out the existing `tisamenus` (son of Orestes), ending the Pelopid rule of Argos/Sparta; the first Return was stayed when the existing `echemus` of Tegea slew Hyllus.
3. **It founds the historical royal lines.** The two Spartan kingships (Agiad/Eurypontid) descend from Aristodemus' twins; the Messenian kings from Cresphontes.

## 7. Conclusion

The Return is the bridge from myth into the Dorian historical age: Heracles' great-great-grandsons cast lots for the Peloponnese, Temenus taking Argos, Cresphontes Messenia, and the twins of Aristodemus Sparta, while the Aetolian Oxylus is paid with Elis and the Achaean Tisamenus, last of the Pelopids, is driven out. Build it as one batch onto the standing `hyllus`/`heracles`/`deianira`, wire the conquest to the existing `tisamenus`/`echemus`, suffix the `aepytus`/`merope` homonyms, and keep Aristodemus' disputed death and the lot-casting as sourced prose.
